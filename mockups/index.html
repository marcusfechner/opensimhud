Fake Monza race, 26 cars in GT3 and GT4. Every overlay reads the same simulated telemetry and is drawn by one render loop. Each overlay updates at its own rate, from 1 Hz for the lap graph up to 60 Hz for inputs and radar. The Race and Practice screens are two example layouts; "All overlays" shows every overlay on its own.
